Local-only call recording

Record calls without Git or Terminal.

Pick your computer. Download the desktop recorder. Open it, press Start, then Stop + Save. Your recordings stay on your device.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

Unsigned pilot builds: macOS Gatekeeper and Windows SmartScreen warnings are expected until production signing is added.

1

Open the app

Double-click the Mac DMG or Windows setup wizard. No developer tools, no GitHub, no Terminal.

2

Press Start

The desktop app has one obvious recording button and a visible local-only status panel.

3

Stop + Save

When the call ends, click Stop + Save. The video stays local on the computer.

Privacy promise

  • No cloud backend.
  • No analytics.
  • No telemetry.
  • No upload path.
  • Desktop exports are local MP4 files.
